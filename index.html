Common questions

Click a question to open it.

How do I book a room?

Search for a free room or open the Rooms page, click "Book this room", fill the form and send the request.

Why was my booking blocked?

Someone already has that room at an overlapping time. Try the Timetable page to see free hours.

Who approves requests?

Only the admin, after logging in, can approve or reject pending requests.

What is the longest booking?

Four hours per request, between 8 AM and 7 PM.
